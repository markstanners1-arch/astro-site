Orion, together

Many small telescopes, one deep picture. Shoot the Orion Nebula this winter with whatever telescope you have, send your frames, and they'll be combined into a single image credited to everyone who took part.

The idea

Light adds up

Everyone shoots M42

Seestar, Dwarf, Draco, Vespera, or your own telescope and camera. From a dark site or the back garden, any night between now and mid-January.

Frames are combined

Every frame is checked, each person's set is stacked on its own, then all of them are aligned and weighted by quality into one image.

One picture, all credited

Twenty telescopes at three hours each is sixty hours of light: more depth than any one of them could reach. Everyone who took part is named.

What to shoot

Your part

Target
M42, the Orion Nebula (M43 and the Running Man in frame are a bonus)
Amount
At least 30 minutes; more is better, over as many nights as you like
Filters
Any: no filter, light-pollution or dual-band. Just say which when you upload
Core frames
Optional but valuable: 20–50 short frames (5–10 s) for the bright core, which burns out in long exposures
When
Now to 17 January. The darkest runs are about 4–13 Nov, 2–12 Dec and 30 Dec – 9 Jan; filtered frames are fine in moonlight
Files
Your individual frames as FITS files (.fit or .fits), not the telescope's own stacked picture

Sending your frames

How to take part

Use the upload box

On the Send your frames box, enter the project code orion26, your name, email and scope, and put M42 Orion together as the target.

Pick your folder

Choose the folder that holds your frames. Short core frames can go in the same upload or a second one with the same name and target.

That's it

You'll get a confirmation, and a short email if anything in your data needs a word. Send more nights any time before the deadline.

What you get

For everyone who takes part

  • The finished picture at full size, to keep, print or share with the credit.
  • A free night report on your own frames: focus, cloud, guiding and more, as plain observations.
  • Your name, or the name you choose, on the credits list on this site.
  • An invitation to the reveal at a Newcastle Astronomical Society meeting in February, and online for those further away.

The fine print

Fair terms

  • Your frames stay yours. They're used only for this project and deleted 30 days after the reveal.
  • The combined picture is credited to all contributors, by the name each chooses (full name, initials or handle), with "Processing: Gosforth Skies".
  • Gosforth Skies may show the picture on this site and on social media. It isn't sold; if prints are ever offered, that's agreed with contributors first.
  • Locations are shown at county or country level only.
  • Combining adds depth, not sharpness: the picture is as sharp as the better telescopes and nights allow.
