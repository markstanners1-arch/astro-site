Your data, kept small

Gosforth Skies is run by one person, Mark Stanners, a sole trader in Newcastle upon Tyne. I collect only what I need to process your pictures, send them to you and, if you buy something, keep the records the law asks for. I never sell or share your data for marketing.

Who to contact

Mark Stanners, trading as Gosforth Skies, Newcastle upon Tyne, UK. Email skies@gosforthskies.co.uk. I'm the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law.

What I collect, and why

Your frames
The raw files you upload, and what you type with them (target, scope, where you took them). Used only to process your pictures and write your night report.
Name and email
To reply to you, send your review page and download link, and credit you if you say I can.
Your picks and notes
What you choose and write on your review page, so I can make the versions you want.
Order details
For a print: your name and delivery address, to have it printed and posted. For any purchase: what you bought, the price and the date.
Payments
Card payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure pages. I never see or store your card number; I see only that you paid, how much, and the email you used.

The legal basis is that I need this information to do what you've asked me to do (a contract with you), and, for payment records, to meet my tax obligations. If I ever want to show your picture on this site, I ask you first, and you can say no or change your mind later.

How long I keep it

Raw frames
Deleted 7 days after you first download your finished picture, or after 30 days if the link is never opened. Tell me if you'd like them kept longer, for example to add more nights.
Download files
Cleared on the same timetable as your frames.
Finished picture
I keep a copy, with your name and email, so I can send it again if you lose it. Ask and I'll delete it.
Emails
Kept while we're in touch, and cleared out when no longer needed.
Sales records
Kept for 6 years, as HMRC requires.

Who else handles it

A few companies help me run the service. Each handles your data only to provide its part, under its own data protection commitments:

  • Cloudflare: stores uploads and downloads, and forwards email sent to gosforthskies.co.uk addresses.
  • Google: my email (Gmail) and file storage.
  • Stripe: takes card payments and keeps the payment records.
  • GitHub: hosts this website. Like any web host it may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • A professional print lab (for print orders): receives the print file and your name and delivery address, to print and post your order.

Some of these companies store data outside the UK (for example in the US or EU), using the safeguards UK law requires for such transfers.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The fonts are served from this site, so visiting doesn't contact Google. When you pay, Stripe's checkout page may use its own cookies to keep the payment secure; that's covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

What's shown publicly

Only with your permission. If your picture appears on the Customer work page, it carries the name you choose (full name, initials or a handle) and a location no more precise than county or country.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. Email me and I'll reply within a month, usually much sooner. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, please tell me first; you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
